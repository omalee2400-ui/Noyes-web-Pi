Privacy Policy

This page describes the current website configuration and planned processing. It will be updated before real form submissions begin.

1. Who is responsible for this website

This legal information website is part of the Noyes & Associates LLC web project, a law firm serving individuals in Connecticut. For questions, call +1 203-572-4748 or visit noyeslawyers.com. The firm must confirm the appropriate privacy-request contact details before launch.

2. Information requested by the form

The evaluation form asks about the type of accident, the state where it occurred, approximate timing, whether injuries occurred, name, phone number, optional email address, preferred language, and permission to contact you. Do not submit Social Security numbers, medical records, identity documents, or other sensitive information in the available fields.

3. Current form status

This website version is still being tested. Based on the current code, the form does not transmit submissions or store answers on a firm-controlled server. If you attempt to submit it, the website displays an informational notice instead. It should not be used to send a real legal inquiry until secure delivery has been configured and tested.

4. Intended purposes if submissions are enabled

Once submissions are enabled, information may be used to review accident or personal injury inquiries, route requests to the appropriate team member, contact individuals who have consented, protect site security, and keep appropriate records of an inquiry. Any additional advertising use must be clearly disclosed before it takes place.

5. Service providers and transfers

The project uses GitHub Pages for hosting, Google Fonts for typography. The icons are embedded directly in the page. External hosting and font providers may receive technical information such as IP addresses and browser details when resources load. No email or CRM provider has yet been selected to receive legal inquiries; the policy must be updated with the actual providers and terms before submissions are activated.

6. Cookies and analytics

The reviewed website code does not itself include a Meta Pixel or Google Analytics. Hosting and third-party resource providers may still maintain technical access logs. If analytics, advertising, non-essential cookies, or conversion events are introduced, the website information and applicable consent mechanisms will be updated before deployment.

7. Retention and security

Once contact processing is enabled, retention periods, access permissions, backups, and technical safeguards must be defined based on the firm’s operational and legal requirements. No specific retention period or technical method is promised until confirmed. No Internet-based system is completely secure.

8. Privacy inquiries

You may ask about the handling of your information or raise a privacy concern using the firm’s published contact channels. Access, correction, deletion, or other rights depend on applicable law and relevant legal or professional obligations.

9. Communications and attorney-client relationship

Submitting information through this website does not create an attorney-client relationship or guarantee representation. Do not send confidential documents, sensitive details, or urgent requests through this testing form. For time-sensitive legal matters, contact the firm or a qualified attorney directly.

10. Updates and effective status

Draft date: October 28, 2026. This is a preliminary policy for a testing website. Noyes & Associates LLC must confirm providers and procedures and arrange legal review before adopting it as a final privacy policy.

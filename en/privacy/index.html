Privacy Policy

This notice explains how information is handled when you request a free accident or personal injury evaluation, including the intake form, security verification, and scheduling a call.

1. Who is responsible

Noyes & Associates LLC is the law firm responsible for inquiries submitted through this personal injury website. For questions about privacy or to reach our team, call +1 (203) 572-4748 or use the contact information published at noyeslawyers.com. This notice applies to this landing page and its intake form; other firm services may have additional notices.

2. Information we collect

When you complete the evaluation, we request your full name, telephone number, email address, preferred language, permission to contact you, and answers about the accident type, the state where it occurred, when it happened, and whether you received care for an injury. An email address is required for this form. We may also record the general source of the inquiry and a campaign reference, when available.

To protect the service and verify submissions, we process technical information, a temporary Cloudflare Turnstile response, and a unique submission identifier. The rate-limiting system uses a cryptographically derived identifier based on an IP address; the code reviewed does not store the raw IP address in that rate-limit table. Hosting providers and other services may maintain their own technical logs under their notices.

Do not enter Social Security numbers, detailed diagnoses, medical records, case numbers, identification documents, or confidential files into this form.

3. How we use information

  • Receive and review personal injury or accident inquiries.
  • Carry out preliminary operational screening and route requests for staff review.
  • Contact you using the details you provide and, if requested, offer times for an initial call.
  • Evaluate whether an out-of-state accident may be suitable for review for a possible attorney referral.
  • Prevent spam, abuse, duplicate submissions, and technical failures.
  • Keep appropriate inquiry records and satisfy applicable legal or professional responsibilities.

The legal basis for processing depends on the circumstances and applicable law, including your permission to be contacted where relevant.

4. How inquiries are screened

The form applies preliminary internal screening criteria based on your answers and may assign different review priorities. This is not a final legal determination, does not provide legal advice, and does not mean that the firm has accepted your case. Staff must review the inquiry before deciding what happens next.

If an accident occurred outside Connecticut, the inquiry may be flagged for possible referral review. This form does not automatically send your details to outside law firms. If a referral is appropriate, we will explain the next steps and obtain additional permission when required before sharing personal information.

5. Service providers

  • GitHub Pages: hosting the public website.
  • Supabase: processing and storage of intake submissions, evaluations, and form-related technical records.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: human verification and abuse prevention.
  • Calendly: availability display and booking management if you choose to schedule a call.
  • Google Fonts: loading the website typography.

Each provider may process technical or usage information as needed to provide its service, subject to its notices. See Supabase, Cloudflare Turnstile, Calendly, and Google.

6. When information may be shared

Access to inquiries should be limited to authorized team members and technology providers involved in processing them. We may disclose information as necessary to respond to an inquiry, comply with applicable law, or respond to valid legal requests.

A potential referral to an outside attorney or firm is handled separately and, where appropriate, with your permission. Submitting an inquiry does not mean a referral has been made or that another firm represents you.

7. Scheduling and Calendly

Once a submission is accepted by Supabase, you may access the embedded Calendly scheduler. To facilitate booking, the website may provide Calendly with the name and email address you entered and technical submission information. When you schedule, Calendly may request more information and process the appointment under its own privacy notice. The scheduling service may send confirmations or reminders.

Showing the scheduler does not mean the firm has accepted your case. Internal appointment verification occurs separately.

8. Cookies, analytics, and external technologies

As of October 30, 2026, the reviewed website source does not include an active proprietary implementation of Meta Pixel, Meta Conversions API, or Google Analytics. The site does use third-party technologies required for form verification and scheduling, which may use technical data, cookies, or similar mechanisms subject to their notices and settings.

If we later add behavior-based advertising, nonessential analytics, or conversion measurement, this notice will be updated and any legally required notice or choice mechanisms will be implemented. Completing a legal inquiry does not by itself mean the information will be used for advertising.

9. Data location and international processing

Technology services may involve handling or storing information outside Connecticut or the country from which you visit, depending on each provider’s infrastructure. The firm should verify contracted storage regions, vendor agreements, and any necessary safeguards before treating this notice as final.

10. Retention and security

Inquiry information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to review the request, manage follow-up, meet applicable legal or professional obligations, and resolve technical issues. The exact retention period and deletion schedule remain subject to formal confirmation by the firm. Until that is approved, this notice does not state a potentially inaccurate number of days.

The website uses HTTPS and server-side validation before confirming a submission. Access to inquiry information should be limited to authorized functions. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

11. Your privacy requests and rights

Depending on your residence, the type of information, and applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or information about use, and to object to certain processing. We may need to verify your identity or retain specific records where legal or professional obligations require it.

Use the contact options in “Who is responsible” to submit a request. Connecticut privacy laws, including the Connecticut Data Privacy Act as amended, may provide specific rights and duties when applicable to the firm and the relevant processing. Not all rights apply in the same way to every inquiry.

12. Information about minors

This initial inquiry form is generally intended for adults. If an inquiry involves a minor’s injury, it should be submitted by an appropriate parent, guardian, or legal representative. Please avoid including sensitive details about a child in the public form fields.

13. Confidentiality and attorney-client relationship

Submitting this form, receiving a response, or scheduling a call does not by itself establish an attorney-client relationship or guarantee that the firm will take a case or obtain a particular result. Submitted information may be reviewed to determine next steps. Do not upload confidential files or detailed medical information through this form.

If you have a court deadline, an emergency, or an urgent need for legal advice, contact a qualified attorney directly rather than relying solely on this form.

14. Updates and effective date

Last updated: October 30, 2026. We will review this notice when the form, service providers, purposes, or legal requirements change. The version published here will state its latest update date.
